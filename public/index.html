ON-DEVICE VOICE

Think out loud.

OpenAI's open-source Whisper transcribes on your device. No speech API, no account, no extra cost.

First use downloads about 100 MB of model files. After that, they're cached in this browser.

Audio stays here. In voice chat, finishing a recording sends its transcript to your chat model. Replies use installed local device voices.

 YOUR WORKSPACE, EXPLAINED

Yours to build on.

WALL-G is the face. Hermes is the engine. Your agent runs on this laptop; its selected AI model may run with an online provider.

Conversations that pick up where you left off

Chat history is saved in this browser and in Hermes. Deleting a chat here does not erase Hermes memory or provider records.

Files with a little more context

PDFs and text are read locally. Files are saved in the project's .local/uploads folder. Extracted text and image content go to your configured model when you send. Image understanding depends on its vision support. Scanned PDFs need OCR first.

Voice without another subscription

Whisper runs entirely on-device after a one-time model download. Audio is never sent to a speech service. Only the transcript enters your chat. Spoken replies use local system voices, when available.

Make WALL-G your own: edit SOUL.md, then send another message. No restart needed.
